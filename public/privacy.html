This notice covers registration for The Becoming Conference on December 4, 2026.

Information collected and its purpose

The registration form collects the attendee’s name, email address, WhatsApp number, school, age, and date of birth, along with their guardian’s email address and phone number. These details are used to manage registrations, identify attendees, apply the age-related consent requirement, and communicate about the conference with attendees or their guardians. The system also records the registration time and the consent role, time, and notice version.

Consent and attendees under 18

An attendee aged 18 or older may provide their own consent. For an attendee under 18, a parent or legal guardian must complete the form and consent on the attendee’s behalf. Providing a guardian’s contact details alone is not consent. The form records the submitter’s declaration; it does not independently verify their identity or legal authority. Please ensure that the guardian is aware their details are being provided.

Access and protection

Registration data is stored in the conference database. Authorized administrators can view registrations and download an export for conference administration. Hosting and database providers process the information as needed to provide the service. Passwords are hashed, administrative access requires authentication, and production connections must use HTTPS. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Retention

Information should be kept only for conference administration and any applicable legal obligations, then deleted securely from the database, downloaded exports, and backups according to the organizers’ retention schedule. The organizers must establish and publish the retention period before opening registration.

Questions, corrections, or withdrawal

Contact the organizer through the communication channel that shared this registration link to request access, correction, deletion, or withdrawal of consent, subject to applicable requirements. The organizers must publish their responsible organization’s identity and a dedicated privacy contact before opening registration.

Before registration opens

The responsible organizers must finalize the privacy contact, retention period, applicable legal requirements, provider arrangements, and any additional guardian verification required. This notice does not claim that the consent checkbox alone satisfies every legal requirement.
